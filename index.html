Website Health

This dashboard checks all our websites around the clock and shows which ones are up, which are slow and which are down. Use it on a wall screen to see problems at a glance, or open Analytics to look back at how a site did over the last day, week or month.

Reading the numbers

A site counts as up when it answers with a normal page or a redirect (status 200 to 399). Anything else, including no answer at all, counts as down and starts an incident.

  • Uptime of 99.9% or more is good.
  • Uptime between 99% and 99.9% needs a look.
  • Uptime below 99% is a problem.

Response times in the charts are capped at 5 seconds, so one very slow check does not flatten the rest of the graph.

Where the data comes from

The sites come from the Plesk server, and a few internal domains are hidden on purpose. Results are refreshed about once a minute, so many people can watch at the same time without overloading the sites. The small graphs on the Live page cover the last 8 hours. Analytics and Incidents keep 30 days of history. Checks are stored while someone has the Live page open, so leave it running on a screen for the best charts.
